Technical solution overview

How the AI system simplifies complex healthcare policies.

The platform pairs a T5 summarizer with automated question generation and a fine-tuned BERT answering layer, wrapped in a user-friendly product experience for healthcare policy education.

System architecture

End-to-end document understanding designed for policy clarity.

Document intake

Upload policy text through drag-and-drop or copy/paste, then clean formatting and segment sections for inference.

NLP processing

Run T5 summarization, generate user-focused questions, and answer them with a healthcare-tuned BERT model.

Presentation layer

Show summaries, highlighted answers, and policy takeaways in plain language with a guided interface.

Model stack

Purpose-built components for health policy interpretation.

Summarization

T5 pipeline

Creates concise, readable summaries from long policy passages while preserving obligations, timelines, and compliance language.

  • Handles lengthy policy text.
  • Produces concise plain-language output.
  • Supports quick overview generation.
Question generation

Relevant follow-up prompts

Identifies the questions a general user is most likely to ask after reading a technical healthcare policy.

  • Improves discoverability of key details.
  • Encourages guided exploration.
  • Surfaces people, actions, and deadlines.
Answering

Fine-tuned BERT

Delivers accurate answers grounded in the uploaded document, helping users understand what a policy actually requires.

  • Targets healthcare and policy language.
  • Returns focused contextual answers.
  • Supports trustworthy interpretation.

Interface design

A user-friendly workflow that reduces cognitive load.

Step-by-step workspace

Users move through upload, summary review, question exploration, and answer inspection in one clear path.

Accessible outputs

Key sections are labeled clearly so non-technical users can distinguish summaries, questions, and answers instantly.

Healthcare-first language

The wording and examples are designed specifically for policy communication in healthcare settings.

Practical value

Helps patients, administrators, and community partners quickly understand obligations and implications.
